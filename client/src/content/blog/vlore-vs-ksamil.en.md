Ask ten people where to spend summer in southern Albania and you'll get ten answers — but two names always repeat: **Vlorë** and **Ksamil**. Both have sea. That's where the resemblance ends.

## One talks, the other whispers

Vlorë has a voice. The promenade hums late, tables spill onto the pavement, and after the beach there's somewhere to *go* — not just somewhere to sleep. It's where you end the day with fresh fish and start the night without a plan.

Ksamil whispers. Small coves, water as still as a pool, and islands that look hand-placed for effect. You come here to slow down, not to stay out till dawn.

## Which one your wallet prefers

Simple rule: Vlorë gives you **more choice at every budget**, while Ksamil climbs the closer August gets. But don't anchor on a price you saw last week — agencies change offers weekly. That's why the live prices above come straight from real offers, not from this page.

## When it comes down to it

First time and want a safe bet? **Vlorë** won't let you down. Already seen that turquoise photo and can't shake it? **Ksamil** — just don't wait for August to book.
