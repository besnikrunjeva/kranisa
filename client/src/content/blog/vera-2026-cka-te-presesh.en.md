Every summer has its fashion, and 2026 is no exception. Here's what we're seeing — and how to avoid the mistake of people who book in a panic.

## The south won't give up the throne

**Sarandë**, **Ksamil** and **Vlorë** stay the first pick for families from Kosovo, and it's not only about the sea: proximity, language and price make them hard to beat — especially for those quick 3–5 night trips you decide on Friday and start on Saturday.

## Turkey plays the value card

Add it all up — flight, hotel, food — and **Antalya** often works out cheaper than a week you organise yourself down south. That's why it comes back every June like a song stuck in your head.

## How to read prices without getting burned

The trick isn't hunting the lowest price; it's recognising a good offer when you see one. Offers change constantly — agencies add new spots and pull sold-out ones within a day. The live prices below update themselves. When a fresh offer that fits appears, that's the moment to act — not next week.
