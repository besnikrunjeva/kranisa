Antalya tops the list every summer, and not without reason — but reduce it to "an all-inclusive resort" and you miss half of it. Here's what's worth leaving the sun lounger for.

## Kaleiçi — the town hiding behind the harbour

Stone lanes, Roman gates and a little port where the sun sets behind the boats: **Kaleiçi** is the first place to get lost on purpose. Go early morning or after the heat breaks — midday here burns.

## The beaches, minus the marketing

- **Konyaalti** — pebbles, clean water and mountains right across the bay. The calmer one.
- **Lara** — golden sand and nearly every big resort. You're here for comfort, not solitude.

## Where Antalya gets interesting

The **Düden** waterfalls that drop straight into the sea, the **Tünektepe** cable car for a view no camera holds, and a day at the ancient city of **Perge** — where you walk 2,000-year-old streets with no crowd overhead.
